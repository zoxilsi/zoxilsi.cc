Colophon

How this is put together.

Typography

Set in IBM Plex Mono by IBM, with handwritten notes in Reenie Beanie by James Grieshaber. Both are self-hosted and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Construction

Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework. Served as static pages, with a small Node backend for the shared visitor count. Source lives on GitHub.

Appearance

Light, Dark, and Blue themes, plus a grain intensity control, live in the “Appearance” panel at the bottom of every page. Your choice is remembered on this device only.

Inspirations

  • henribredt.de — the quiet, typographic layout this redesign borrows from.
